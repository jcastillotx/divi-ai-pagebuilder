## Getting Started

### Accessing AI Features

After installation and configuration, access AI features in two ways:

1. **From Divi Builder Toolbar**
   - Open any page in Divi Builder (Visual or Backend)
   - Click the **AI** button in the top toolbar
   - The AI Creation Wizard opens

2. **From WordPress Admin**
   - Go to **Divi AI > Create New**
   - Select your creation type
   - Follow the wizard steps

### First-Time Setup Checklist

Before creating your first AI-generated content:

- [ ] API keys configured (Settings > Divi AI > AI Providers)
- [ ] Brand colors set (Appearance > Customize > Divi AI Global Styles)
- [ ] Brand fonts selected (Appearance > Customize > Divi AI Global Styles)
- [ ] Company logo uploaded (if using Site Setup)

### Creating a Full Page

Create complete page layouts with multiple sections, all styled to your brand.

#### Step 1: Select Page Type

Choose the type of page you want to create:

| Page Type | Best For |
|-----------|----------|
| **Landing Page** | Marketing campaigns, product launches |
| **Home Page** | Main website entry point |
| **About Us** | Company information, team |
| **Services** | Service offerings, features |
| **Contact** | Contact forms, location info |
| **Portfolio** | Work samples, case studies |
| **Blog/News** | Article listings |
| **Pricing** | Plans and pricing tables |
| **FAQ** | Frequently asked questions |

#### Step 2: Describe Your Layout

Provide details about what you need:

**Example prompts:**

> "A modern landing page for a SaaS product with a hero section featuring a product screenshot, three feature highlights with icons, testimonials from customers, pricing comparison table, and a final call-to-action."

> "Professional services page for a law firm with an introduction, practice areas grid, attorney profiles, case results statistics, and contact form."

> "E-commerce home page with hero banner, featured products carousel, category showcase, trust badges, and newsletter signup."

#### Step 3: Specify Media Needs

Tell the AI about images and media:

- **Stock Photos**: "Need professional business photos"
- **AI Generated**: "Generate abstract tech backgrounds"
- **Patterns**: "Use geometric design patterns"
- **Your Media**: "I'll upload my own images"

#### Step 4: Review and Insert

- Preview the generated page with your brand styling
- Make adjustments if needed
- Click **Insert to Page** to add to your Divi layout

### Creating a Section

Add individual sections to existing pages quickly.

#### Step 1: Select Section Type

| Section Type | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Hero** | Large banner with headline and CTA |
| **Features** | Feature boxes or cards |
| **Services** | Service offerings display |
| **Testimonials** | Customer reviews and quotes |
| **Team** | Team member profiles |
| **Pricing** | Pricing tables and plans |
| **CTA** | Call-to-action blocks |
| **FAQ** | Accordion Q&A sections |
| **Gallery** | Image galleries |
| **Contact** | Contact forms and info |
| **Stats** | Numbers and counters |
| **Blog Posts** | Recent articles grid |

#### Step 2: Background Options

Choose how to style your section background:

**AI-Generated Image**
> "Create a professional office environment with modern furniture and natural lighting"

**AI-Generated Design**
> "Abstract gradient with purple and blue tones"
> "Geometric pattern with brand colors"
> "Subtle wave pattern in light gray"

**Solid Color**
- Uses your brand colors automatically
- Options: Primary, Secondary, Light, Dark

#### Step 3: Describe Content

Be specific about what content you need:

**Good example:**
> "Three feature cards highlighting our main services: Web Design, SEO, and Digital Marketing. Each card should have an icon, headline, brief description, and a learn more link."

**Better example:**
> "Feature section for a dental clinic with four services: General Dentistry, Cosmetic Dentistry, Orthodontics, and Emergency Care. Use calming, professional tone. Include brief descriptions focusing on patient comfort and modern techniques."

#### Step 4: Preview and Insert

- Review the generated section
- Adjust content if needed
- Insert into your current page

### Site Setup

Configure your website's global elements: header, footer, and 404 page.

#### Step 1: Header Selection

Browse professional header templates:

- **Classic**: Logo left, menu right
- **Centered**: Logo centered, menu below
- **Fullscreen**: Hidden menu with hamburger
- **Mega Menu**: Multi-column dropdowns
- **Transparent**: Overlay on hero sections

After selection, the AI will:
- Insert your company logo
- Add your primary navigation menu
- Apply your brand colors and fonts

#### Step 2: Footer Selection

Choose from footer styles:

- **Simple**: Logo, copyright, social links
- **Multi-Column**: Multiple widget areas
- **Full-Width**: Wide layout with CTA
- **Minimal**: Copyright only
- **Contact Footer**: Contact info prominent

#### Step 3: 404 Page Selection

Select a custom 404 error page:

- **Friendly**: Warm, helpful message
- **Illustrated**: Custom graphics
- **Search Focused**: Prominent search bar
- **Minimal**: Simple redirect options

#### Step 4: Logo and Menu Configuration

**Logo Upload**
- Upload your logo image
- AI will automatically place it in header and footer
- Supports PNG, JPG, SVG formats

**Menu Configuration**
- Select existing menu or create new
- If no menu exists, AI creates one from:
  - Published pages
  - Key sections of your site

#### Step 5: Apply to Theme Builder

The AI automatically:
1. Creates Theme Builder templates
2. Assigns header to all pages
3. Assigns footer to all pages
4. Assigns 404 template to error pages

## Tips for Best Results

### Writing Effective Prompts

**Be Specific**
- Bad: "Make a nice page"
- Good: "Create a landing page for a fitness app with hero section, three feature cards, testimonial slider, and pricing table"

**Include Context**
- Bad: "Services section"
- Good: "Services section for a digital marketing agency targeting small businesses, highlighting SEO, PPC, and Social Media services"

**Mention Style**
- Bad: "Modern design"
- Good: "Clean, minimalist design with lots of white space, subtle shadows, and rounded corners"

**Specify Tone**
- Bad: "Some text about us"
- Good: "Professional yet approachable about us content that builds trust with potential clients"

### Iterating on Results

1. **Start Broad**: Generate initial layout
2. **Review Structure**: Check section order and flow
3. **Refine Content**: Edit text for your voice
4. **Adjust Styling**: Fine-tune colors and spacing
5. **Add Media**: Replace placeholders with your images

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Too Vague**: Give enough detail for the AI to work with
- **Ignoring Brand**: Always set up Global Styles first
- **One-and-Done**: Plan to iterate and refine
- **Skipping Preview**: Always preview before inserting

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need both OpenAI and Anthropic API keys?**
A: No, you only need one. OpenAI is recommended for most use cases. Anthropic can be configured as a fallback.

**Q: Will AI-generated content hurt my SEO?**
A: The AI generates high-quality, unique content. However, we recommend reviewing and personalizing content for best SEO results.

**Q: Can I edit AI-generated content?**
A: Yes! Once inserted, all content is fully editable in Divi Builder like any other module.

**Q: How are API costs calculated?**
A: Costs depend on your API provider's pricing. Check Settings > Divi AI > Usage & Limits to monitor usage.

### Template Questions

**Q: Can I use templates without AI?**
A: Yes, you can browse and insert templates without using AI content generation.

**Q: How do templates adapt to my brand?**
A: Templates use a Design Token System that maps template colors and fonts to your Global Styles settings.

**Q: Can I save my own templates?**
A: Currently, the plugin uses the curated library. Custom template support is planned for future versions.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Is my content stored on external servers?**
A: Content prompts are sent to AI providers (OpenAI/Anthropic) for generation. Generated content is stored in your WordPress database.

**Q: What happens if the API is unavailable?**
A: The plugin gracefully degrades - you can still use templates and manual Divi features.

**Q: Can multiple users use the plugin?**
A: Yes, usage is tracked per user. Administrators can set rate limits in settings.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: Templates aren't loading my brand colors**
A: Ensure Global Styles are configured in Appearance > Customize > Divi AI Global Styles

**Q: AI generation is taking too long**
A: Complex requests may take 15-30 seconds. Check your internet connection and API status.

**Q: I'm getting rate limit errors**
A: Check Settings > Divi AI > Usage & Limits. You may need to wait or increase limits.
